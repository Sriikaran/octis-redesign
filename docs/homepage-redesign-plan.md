## 1. Executive Direction & Guiding Principles

As explicitly stated in `website.docx`:

> *"Changes to the existing agency content — preserve the current structure and wording unless specifically changed below."*  
> *"IMPORTANT — DO NOT REWRITE THE WHOLE WEBSITE"*  
> *"Keep the agency's existing website structure, sections, SEO structure, feature descriptions, FAQs, CTAs and overall copy. Apply only the changes listed in this document. The objective is to strengthen Octis positioning, especially AI, without replacing the agency's work unnecessarily."*

### Key Rules of Engagement:
1. **Additive & Targeted**: Retain the completed high-fidelity homepage clone (`Hero.tsx`, `Features.tsx`, `SpotlightSection.tsx`, `Compliance.tsx`, `Pricing.tsx`, `Faq.tsx`, `CtaBanner.tsx`, `Footer.tsx`, `Navbar.tsx`). Modify only the specific copy and elements requested.
2. **AI-Powered Positioning**: Elevate Octis from simple jewellery management software to an **AI-Powered Jewellery ERP** and **Jewellery Intelligence** platform.
3. **No Fabricated Data**: Adhere strictly to verified facts and copy. Section 15 explicitly flags unverified credibility claims (e.g. 500+ Jewellers, 10,000+ Transactions) for verification rather than blind publication.
4. **Preserve SEO Foundation**: Retain existing H1/H2 hierarchy, semantic tags, and keywords ("Jewellery Management Software for Indian Jewellery Retailers") while integrating new phrases ("AI-Powered Jewellery ERP", "Jewellery Business Intelligence").

## 4. Architectural Boundaries

- **Public Marketing Website Only**: All changes are confined to `src/app/page.tsx` and public marketing components under `src/components/`.
- **Preserved Baseline**: The original agency clone implemented in Phases 4A–4C remains intact and recoverable in Git history.
- **ERP Work Decoupled**: The `src/app/(erp)/` route group and `src/components/erp/` primitives remain preserved in the codebase but are completely out of scope for this homepage task.
- **Zero Hallucination**: No fake customer logos, testimonials, or claims will be added. All copy originates directly from `website.docx`.
